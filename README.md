# 🐝 BeeHive: Build the Habit. Find Your Freedom.

> *"I didn't build a budgeting app. I built a tool to help people change their lives, one day at a time."*

---

## Why BeeHive Exists

Most people know they should track their spending. Very few actually do.

Not because they're lazy, but because the tools we've built for them feel like chores. Cold spreadsheets. Judgment-filled dashboards. Apps that show you how much you failed last month.

I started BeeHive with a different question:

**What if tracking money felt like taking care of something you loved?**

Bees build their hive drop by drop, day by day. They don't panic about the whole hive. They just show up and do their part, consistently. I believe humans work the same way. The path to financial freedom isn't about dramatic sacrifice. It's about small, repeated actions that compound over time.

BeeHive is my attempt to build a tool that makes those small actions feel meaningful, and over time, transformative.

---

## The Bigger Mission

This app is not a finished product. It's the beginning of a long-term research project.

I want to understand:
- What makes financial habits actually stick?
- What kinds of feedback loops help people feel progress, not guilt?
- How do we make abstract goals (like "financial freedom") feel real and close?

BeeHive is my first attempt to answer those questions through design. I plan to keep improving it based on real user feedback, behavioral research, and honest reflection on what works and what doesn't.

If you use this app and want to share your experience (what helped, what didn't, what you wish it did differently) I genuinely want to hear from you. This project is, at its core, about people.

## What BeeHive Does

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🍯 **Honey Streak** | Daily logging streak that builds momentum and makes consistency feel rewarding |
| 💸 **Expense Tracking** | Simple, fast logging across 7 categories: Eat / Live / Wear / Enjoy / Edu / Ride / Other |
| 📊 **Spending Overview** | Date-range charts and category breakdowns to understand your patterns |
| 🏦 **Assets Tracker** | Track savings, investments, and retirement funds in one place |
| 📋 **Fixed Expenses** | Log recurring monthly costs so your true "available" budget is always visible |
| 🐝 **Freedom Simulator** | Based on the 4% Rule, see exactly when you could achieve financial independence |
| 🌍 **Bilingual** | Full Korean and English support, switchable in-app |
| 📱 **Widget + Notifications** | Home screen widget and daily reminders to keep the habit alive |

---

## Tech Stack

- **Platform**: iOS (SwiftUI)
- **Storage**: UserDefaults + App Group (for widget sync)
- **Localization**: Custom `L()` system with `ko` / `en` Localizable.strings
- **Notifications**: UNUserNotificationCenter
- **Widget**: WidgetKit

## A Note on Design Philosophy

Every decision in BeeHive was made with one question in mind:

> *Does this help someone feel like they're making progress?*

Not performing productivity. Not tracking numbers for numbers' sake. Actually feeling, in their gut, that today's small action matters.

The streak is there because consistency needs to feel alive.
The simulator is there because abstract goals need to feel real.
The honey jar fills up because progress deserves to be seen.

I don't think I've got it right yet. But I'll keep at it.
